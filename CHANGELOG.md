# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-23

First version, built against NEB 1.0.5.

- A key created on the phone or imported, encrypted with a password.
- The balance, read from the project's public node or from a node you set.
- Receive with the address as text and as a QR code. Send with a review window.
- A payment from one phone to another over Bluetooth Low Energy. Both phones keep the signed payment and give it to the network once they have internet.
- With no internet, the wallet signs with the coins it saw last and sets aside the coins of a payment that is waiting for a block.
- Android 10 or later, arm64.
